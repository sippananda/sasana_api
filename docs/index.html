        About the data

        • Source: Department of Environment Malaysia (DOE), Air Pollutant Index Management System, eqms.doe.gov.my.
        • New readings are collected once per hour, at about 15 minutes past the hour.
        • If a station reading is late, one more collection runs about 15 minutes later.
        • A reading that is still missing after that is collected in the next hour.
        • Times are Malaysia time. This page refreshes by itself every 5 minutes.
        • Forecast (section 6): contains modified Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service information (CAMS, run by ECMWF). Weather from ECMWF open data. Both through Open-Meteo. The forecast is made twice a day, at about 7:30 am and 7:30 pm.
